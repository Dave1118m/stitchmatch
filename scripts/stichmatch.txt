Part 1: Functional Requirements 1   User Management (FR-01)  1.1   Functional Requirements  •   Users including both customers and tailors must register via email or through Google and Facebook OAuth  •   Users must select their role as either customer or tailor during the signup process  •   Tailor registration requires approval by the admin to verify craftsmanship before full access is granted  •   Users can view, edit, and delete their own profile, supporting full CRUD operations 3

2   Tailor Discovery & Profiles (FR-02)  2.1   Functional Requirements  •   Tailors must create a profile containing bio, location, specialties such as suits or dresses, portfolio images, and base pricing information  •   Customers can search and filter tailors by location, specialty, price range, and rating  •   Customers can view a tailor’s detailed profile page which includes reviews from previous customers 4

3   Service Request & Agreement (FR-03)  3.1   Functional Requirements  •   Customers send a service request to a tailor describing the garment type, fabric pref- erences, and deadline  •   Tailors receive requests and can either accept, reject, or negotiate through chat  •   Once the tailor accepts, the system moves the request to the under discussion status  •   Both parties must explicitly click confirm agreement to lock the project details includ- ing garment specs, final price, and deadline  •   Status flow follows this sequence: Pending to Under Discussion to Agreed to In Progress to Completed 5

4   Direct Messaging (FR-04)  4.1   Functional Requirements  •   Real-time chat between customer and tailor for the specific request, implemented via WebSocket or polling-based fallback  •   Messaging supports text, images for fabric swatches, and file attachments such as PDFs  •   Chat history is persisted and tied to the specific request ID 6

5   AI Measurement System (FR-05)  5.1   Functional Requirements  •   After agreement, the system prompts the customer to upload photos from front, side, and back per AI requirements  •   The system sends images to the external AI measurement API  •   The API returns key body measurements including chest, waist, hip, inseam, shoulder width, and arm length  •   Measurements are stored securely and displayed only to the associated tailor and cus- tomer  •   Tailors can add manual adjustments to the AI measurements if needed, such as adding 2 centimeters for comfort 7

6   Order Tracking (FR-06)  6.1   Functional Requirements  •   Tailors update the order status with stages such as cutting, sewing, or ready for fitting  •   Customers receive notifications through in-app alerts or email on status changes  •   Tailors can upload progress photos to the order for customer visibility 8

7   Reviews & Ratings (FR-07)  7.1   Functional Requirements  •   Upon completed status, customers can rate the tailor on a 1 to 5 star scale and leave text feedback  •   Tailors can reply to reviews publicly 9
